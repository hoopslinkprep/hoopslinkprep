Logan White commits to Hoops Link Prep

Weeki Wachee's scoring record holder and all-time leading rebounder is joining the Hoops Link Prep post-grad program.

Hoops Link Prep · October 26, 2026

Hoops Link Prep is excited to welcome Logan White to the program. The 6'6 forward averaged 23.4 points and 11.1 rebounds per game his senior season at Weeki Wachee High School, and leaves as the program's scoring record holder and all-time leading rebounder.

Logan joins the Hoops Link Prep post-grad program, based in Trinity, Florida, with training across the greater Tampa Bay area. The program takes a small group of players each year and develops the whole player: skill work, strength and mobility, the mental side of the game, and direct relationships with college coaches.
